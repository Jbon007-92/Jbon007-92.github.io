HomePower

A private home energy app for one household. It reads the home's Tesla Powerwall and its Nest thermostats, and plans when the household's own computers run, so that they use spare solar power and free night-time power. It is not offered to the public: only its owner signs in to it.

Privacy policy

This site also holds HomePower's public key for Tesla's Fleet API and the page Tesla's sign-in returns to.
