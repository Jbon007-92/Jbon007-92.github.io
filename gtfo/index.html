GTFO: Get The Facts Out.

The Platform

Five reforms. No party line on anything else.

Candidates sign the reform pledge and stay free to vote their conscience on everything else. That keeps the door open to people from both sides.

Transparency

Every bill is posted publicly at least 72 hours before a vote. Agency spending is published as searchable open data, and lobbyist meetings are logged.

Fiscal Oversight

Budgets pass on time, or members of Congress lose their pay until they do. Every agency gets regular audits, and taxpayers get a "where your dollar went" receipt.

Ethics

Members of Congress can't trade individual stocks. Anyone leaving office waits five years before they can lobby.

Accountability

No member serves more than 12 years. Every major measure gets a recorded vote, so there are no voice votes on big spending.

The Sunset Rule

New regulations and programs expire after 10 years unless Congress votes to renew them. If something works, it can earn its renewal.

"Show your work!"

The rally chant, and the standard we hold every official to, including our own.

The Candidate System

Get in. Do the job. Get out.

We back statesmen and stateswomen who run on a specific mission, not a career. Each one names the problem they'll fix, sets a deadline, and goes home when the job is done.

  1. 01

    Declare the mission

    Publish 1 to 3 measurable goals, a service limit, and a signed exit date before the campaign starts.

  2. 02

    Pass the Statesman Test

    A real career outside politics, no lobbying history, proof of working across the aisle, and a recorded public Q&A defending the plan.

  3. 03

    Serve in public view

    A public scorecard is updated every quarter, with town halls every six months. Members who break the pledge lose the endorsement, and we say so publicly.

  4. 04

    Hand off and go home

    The member leaves when the mission is done or the deadline arrives. A lessons-learned report goes to the next candidate, and a 5-year lobbying ban applies.
