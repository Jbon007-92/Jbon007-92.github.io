HomePower privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026.

HomePower is a private home energy app for one household. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner signs in to it.

What it reads

  • From Google, through Google's Smart Device Management API and only with the owner's permission: each Nest thermostat's temperature, humidity, mode, eco setting, whether it is heating or cooling, and its set points. HomePower may later adjust the thermostats, within limits the owner sets.
  • From Tesla, through Tesla's Fleet API and only with the owner's permission: the Powerwall's power and charge readings.

Where it goes

  • The readings stay on the owner's own computers and home network. HomePower uses them to show the owner the home's power and to plan when the owner's own computers run. The owner's own tools on that network, including the owner's AI assistant, can read them when the owner asks.
  • HomePower does not sell, rent, share or hand this data to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.
  • The sign-ins HomePower holds for Google and Tesla are kept encrypted on the owner's computer and are sent only back to Google or Tesla.

Removing its access

  • Google: remove HomePower under Third-party apps and services at https://myaccount.google.com/connections, or press Disconnect Nest on HomePower's own page.
  • Tesla: remove HomePower under your Tesla account's third-party apps.
  • The owner can delete the readings from the owner's computers at any time.

Contact

Use the support address Google shows on HomePower's sign-in screen.
